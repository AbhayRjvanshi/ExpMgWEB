### Two standing policy decisions

1. **Raw text in MySQL, escaping at render time.** The three assertions that
   expect `O&#039;Brien` / `&lt;script&gt;` to be *stored* are rejected:
   write-time escaping double-escapes on edit round-trips, breaks `LIKE` search
   and corrupts exports. Prepared statements plus `esc()` in the UI are the
   correct boundary.
2. **Section 30 is deferred, not rejected.** The `response.php` rewrite plus
   try/catch and the 405/400 contracts is a self-contained change with its own
   regression surface.
- [ ] **`tests/distributed_system_test.php` runs 21/25 standalone**: the four
  failures (global health aggregation, readiness, cache pattern invalidation,
  latency score) come from the dormant distributed stack and are out of scope
  until those services are wired.
- [ ] **No CSRF assertions in the suite**: the CSRF header/cookie/IP-gate paths
  are verified only by ad-hoc probes, so a regression there would not fail CI.
